Why 3.0T MRI Matters

Exceptional anatomical contrast without compromise.

Magnetic Resonance Imaging utilizes a high-homogeneity 3.0 Tesla magnetic field and calibrated radiofrequency pulses to capture precise molecular hydrogen tissue responses. This enables earlier detection of subtle neurological lesions, microvascular pathology, and tendon micro-tears that standard 1.5T systems often miss.

Superior Neurovascular & Brain Detail High-resolution diffusion tensor imaging, brain perfusion, and uncompromised white matter differentiation.
Advanced Cartilage & Orthopedic Mapping T2-mapping of articular cartilage to identify early degenerative changes before surgical thresholds.
Claustrophobia-Friendly 70cm Open Bore Spacious architecture with ambient LED sky lighting and active audio communication throughout every sequence.

Preparation Checklist

How to prepare for your MRI scan

Please review these simple instructions before arriving at Clarity Imaging Center:

1
Leave Metallic Items in Secure Lockers

Jewelry, watches, piercings, belts, and cards with magnetic strips must be safely locked in our private dressing cabins.

2
Disclose Medical Implants & History

Notify our team if you have pacemakers, cochlear implants, aneurysm clips, or recent vascular stents for safety clearance.

3
Fasting Requirements for Contrast Scans

If your examination requires IV gadolinium contrast, please fast for 4 hours prior. Routine non-contrast scans require no fasting.
