A 3.0 Tesla MRI magnet produces twice the magnetic field strength of standard 1.5T scanners. This generates significantly higher spatial resolution and signal contrast, enabling radiologists to visualize microscopic vascular structures, delicate brain tissue, and early cartilage tears that may appear subtle on lower-field systems.

Our 128-slice CT scanner utilizes AI-powered iterative reconstruction and dynamic z-axis collimation. These intelligent systems modulate the X-ray beam in real time based on patient body thickness, reducing ionizing radiation dose by up to 80% while preserving ultra-sharp diagnostic image quality.

We prioritize patient comfort. Our MRI features a 70cm wide-bore design with open ends, ambient adjustable lighting, QuietScan acoustic noise suppression, and continuous two-way intercom communication. You will hold an emergency call bulb, and a family member is welcome in the room following safety screening.

Emergency and STAT scan results are communicated within 2 hours. Routine reports and DICOM imaging series are finalized by our dual-board radiologists within 18–24 hours and uploaded directly to our secure Cloud PACS physician portal. Patients receive a direct download link via SMS and email.
