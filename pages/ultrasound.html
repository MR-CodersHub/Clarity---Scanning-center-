Preparation Checklist

How to prepare for your ultrasound

Preparation varies depending on the specific organ being examined:

1
Upper Abdomen Scans: 6 Hours Fasting

Refrain from eating or drinking tea/coffee for 6 hours prior to allow optimal gall bladder distension and minimize bowel gas.

2
Pelvic & Obstetric Scans: Full Bladder Protocol

Drink 4 to 5 glasses of plain water 1 hour prior to your examination and do not urinate. A full bladder acts as an acoustic window.

3
Thyroid, Scrotal & Doppler: Zero Fasting Needed

Vascular Doppler and superficial soft tissue scans require no dietary restrictions or special fluid preparation.
